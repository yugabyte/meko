# Phase 4a — Stop dual-writing rel pages to layer files

`TimelineWriter::put_batch` now filters relation-page entries out of
the batch handed to `layer.put_batch`; only non-rel keys (SLRU,
metadata, dir listings, aux, repl origins, rel-size sentinels) still
flow into the layer stack.

Batches with no non-rel entries skip the layer call entirely.

**Compaction loop disabled** — `start_background_loops` no longer
spawns `compaction_loop`; image-layer creation has nothing to do
against `pageserver.pages` anyway.
