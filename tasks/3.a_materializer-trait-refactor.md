# Phase 3a — `Materializer` trait refactor

Trait now takes `(key, target_lsn, prior_chain, current)` so
production wrappers don't need a back-reference to `PageWriter`.

`WalRedoMaterializer` (`pageserver/src/page_materializer.rs`) was the
production impl — converted chain rows back into `(base_img, records)`
and called `walredo::PostgresRedoManager::request_redo`. 5 tests
covered the pure decode helper.

Superseded by the design pivot in Phase 3d (walredo moved to a
background materialiser; `page_materializer.rs` was deleted).
