# Phase 4h — Dedicated YSQL connection for internal traffic vs compute reads

Two `YsqlClient`s per pageserver:

- `yb-pageserver:compute_reads` serves `getPage` reads
  (`scan_range`, `read_chain`, `scan_meta_range`, `read_slru_chain`)
  — idle when the cache absorbs them.
- `yb-pageserver:internal` serves walreceiver `PageWriter::flush`
  writes, materialiser `read_chain_durable` cache miss, and
  materialiser image INSERT.

Visible in `pg_stat_activity` as two distinct `application_name`s.
Compute `getPage` stays isolated from a 25 MiB flush or a multi-job
materialiser INSERT and vice versa.

No measurable perf delta on the single-client microbench (cache
already absorbed contention) — the structural isolation is the
point.
