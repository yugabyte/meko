# Phase 3b — Tenant / Timeline plumbing for dual-write

- `TenantSharedResources` carries `Option<Arc<PageStore>>`.
- `Tenant` stashes it; `TimelineResources` passes it through.
- `Timeline` builds `PageWriter` + spawns `FlushTask` when both
  `page_store` and `walredo_mgr` are present.
- `TimelineWriter::put_batch` runs `extract_triples` and forwards to
  `page_writer.put` *before* the layer-file write — so a YB failure
  aborts the whole batch and walingest retries.
- `Timeline::shutdown` drains the `FlushTask` + does a final
  `page_writer.flush()`.

**Reads still went through the layer stack at the end of 3b** —
Phase 3c is the read-flip. Dual-write itself was removed in Phase 4a.
